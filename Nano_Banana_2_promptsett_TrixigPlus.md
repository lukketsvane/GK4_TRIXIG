# Nano Banana 2 — promptsett for Trixig+ redesign

Konsistent visualisering av Trixig+ for sluttpresentasjon, plansje og rapport. Alle promptane er bygde rundt eit felles **stilgrunnlag** som sikrar at lys, materiale, tone og bakgrunn er like på tvers av bilete. Lim stilgrunnlaget framføre kvar einskild prompt før du sender til Nano Banana 2.

---

## 0. Stilgrunnlag (fast preambel)

> **Style:** Premium industrial design product photography. Minimalist studio shot. Soft, diffused daylight from upper left, gentle fill from right, no harsh specular highlights. Subtle ambient occlusion under the object. Background: seamless cool-grey gradient (#EEEEEE at top, #DDDDDD at bottom), no horizon line, no props unless specified. Color: monochromatic black–grey palette with one warm signal-orange accent (Pantone 165 C, ≈#FF6A13) restricted to the direction-switch arrow relief and the active status-LED dot. Material rendering: Trixig+ housing in matte black ABS with a fine textured satin finish (no gloss, no soft-touch rubber overmold), grey POM trigger with slightly cooler tone, brushed satin steel for the 1/4" hex bit holder, exposed neodymium magnet with metallic grey sheen, exposed Torx T10 stainless steel screws with subtle countersunk heads. Camera: full-frame mirrorless, 100mm macro equivalent, f/8, ISO 100, focused on product, slight depth-of-field falloff. Sharp, photorealistic, advertising-grade. No text, no logos, no watermarks unless explicitly requested.

Lim denne preambelen først. Deretter følg ein av promptane under.

## 1. Hero shot — heile produktet, ¾-vinkel

> Subject: a single Trixig+ cordless screwdriver, isolated centered in frame. Form: soft pistol shape, 110-degree grip angle (relaxed, ergonomic, not aggressive), overall length approximately 170 mm, weight ≈350 g. Front: 1/4" hex bit holder in brushed steel with a small exposed neodymium ring magnet visible just behind the chuck, and a single SMD LED bezel at the top of the nose. Body: matte black ABS housing in two clamshell halves joined by six exposed Torx T10 stainless screws (three visible on this side), clean parting line. Trigger: light-grey POM, slight wedge profile, recessed into the grip. Rear: an asymmetric direction switch protruding from the upper rear of the body — the visible side is convex, embossed with a forward-pointing arrow in raised orange relief. Below the grip, in the rear, a tiny USB-C port and a horizontal four-dot battery status LED strip (one orange dot lit, three dim). Camera angle: three-quarter front, slightly above eye level (15° down), object turned 30° to camera-left so the trigger and direction switch both read clearly. Aspect ratio 4:3.

## 2. Sideview ortografisk — for formføringsplansje / strektegning

> Subject: a single Trixig+ cordless screwdriver in strict pure side view (camera dead perpendicular to the long axis, no perspective distortion). Show the full silhouette from bit holder tip at left to grip base at right. Soft pistol form, 110° grip angle. All exposed screws (three on this side) clearly visible as small Torx T10 dots aligned along the parting line. Trigger fully readable as a wedge on the inside of the grip. Direction switch protrudes from upper rear, visible as a convex bump with the orange forward-arrow relief readable. Front nose shows bit holder, magnet ring, and LED bezel. Underside of grip ends with a strap loop. Background: completely flat #EEEEEE, no gradient, no shadow except a single soft contact shadow directly below. Lighting: even, frontal, no directional shading — this image must read like a high-fidelity orthographic technical reference, not a dramatic product shot. Aspect ratio 16:9.

## 3. Front view — bitsfeste-detalj

> Subject: extreme close-up frontal view of the Trixig+ nose. Frame fills with the front face of the screwdriver, looking directly into the 1/4" hex bit holder. Visible: the hex socket cavity (dark, slightly chamfered edge), the brushed steel collar around it, the neodymium ring magnet immediately behind the collar (matte metallic grey, faint annular seam), and one SMD LED at the 12-o'clock position embedded behind a small clear window. The matte black ABS housing fans out around the steel components, with two of the Torx T10 screws visible at lower-left and lower-right of frame. Camera distance close, focal point on hex socket, slight bokeh on outer ABS. Aspect ratio 1:1.

## 4. Bakvy — retningsbrytar, status-LEDs, USB-C

> Subject: a Trixig+ photographed from directly behind, looking at the rear cap. Frame: rear face of the body filling most of the image. Top center: the asymmetric direction switch — the rocker spans roughly 18 mm horizontally, the LEFT side is convex (rounded outward, embossed with a forward-pointing arrow in orange relief), the RIGHT side is concave (recessed inward, embossed with a backward-pointing arrow in dark grey relief, no orange). The asymmetry between the two sides is visually unmistakable. Centered below the rocker: a horizontal row of four 2-mm-diameter status LED dots, the leftmost dot glowing soft orange, the other three dim. Lower center: a small USB-C port set in a recessed pocket. Below USB-C: the discreet wordmark "TRIXIG+" debossed into the ABS, no paint fill. Two Torx T10 screw heads visible at the lower corners. Background neutral grey gradient. Aspect ratio 1:1.

## Bruksrettleiing

**Konsistens.** Bruk alltid stilgrunnlaget (avsnitt 0) som første del av prompten. Det sikrar at lys, materiale, fargekoding og bakgrunn samsvarer på tvers av rendringar.

**Iterasjon.** Om Nano Banana 2 leverer eit bilete der eit detalj er feil (til dømes glossy i staden for matte ABS, eller for store skruer), legg eitt enkelt korreksjonsledd til same prompt: "Correction: housing must be matte, not glossy." Ikkje skriv ein heilt ny prompt — det bryt konsistensen.

**Variasjon.** Skal du ha fire variantar av same skot, kjør same prompt fire gonger med ulike seed-tal i staden for å variera ordlyden.

**Negativ-prompt (om Nano Banana 2 støttar det):** "no glossy plastic, no soft-touch rubber overmold, no extra branding, no text overlays, no fingerprints, no scratches, no chrome, no fluorescent colors except specified orange accent."

**Fargekontroll.** Den oransje aksenten er einaste signalfarge — han er reservert til pil-relieffet på den konvekse retningsbrytarsida og til den aktive status-LED-prikken. Ingen andre delar av produktet skal vera oransje.

**Aspect ratios.** Hero (1) og before/after (11) er 4:3 og 16:9 for plansje. Eksplodert vy (5) er 9:16 for vertikal slide. Detaljmakroar (3, 8, 9, 10) er 1:1 eller 16:9 for plansje-aksentar. Brukskontekst (7, 12) er 3:2 for redaksjonell sluttpresentasjon.

**Levering.** Lagre kvart bilete med navngiving `trixig_plus_<promptnummer>_<seed>.png` for sporbarheit i prosjektmappa. Foreslår undermappe: `prosjektmateriale/visualisering/`.

## 13. Brand-mark — TRIXIG+ wordmark debossa i ABS

> Subject: extreme close-up of the TRIXIG+ wordmark debossed directly into the matte black ABS housing of a Trixig+ screwdriver. Frame fills with the wordmark and a few centimeters of surrounding ABS texture. The wordmark reads "TRIXIG+" in a bold geometric grotesque (Verdana Bold or Akzidenz Grotesk Bold equivalent), 12 mm tall, debossed approximately 0.4 mm below the surface. The T has a small Torx six-point star integrated into the vertical stem, the G has a tiny Pozidriv cross integrated into the inner curve where the letter closes, and the plus sign has four small dots — one in each arm — referencing the four-dot status LED strip elsewhere on the product. No paint fill, no color: the lettering is visible only through the play of light on the depressed surface. Side light from upper-left grazes across the surface, revealing the depth of the debossing and the fine texture of the ABS. Photorealistic, almost forensic. Aspect ratio 16:9.
